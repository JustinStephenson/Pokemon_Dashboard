# Glossary

**Main Pokémon**: The Pokémon the dashboard is currently about. Its artwork is the large image in the middle, and the Info, Statistics and message panels all describe it.

**Neighbour**: The Pokémon directly before (previous Neighbour) or after (next Neighbour) the Main Pokémon in Pokédex order, shown as a small image with an arrow on each side of the Main Pokémon. Clicking a Neighbour makes it the Main Pokémon. The first Pokémon has no previous Neighbour and the last has no next Neighbour. Also called the side buttons.

**Evolution**: The chain of Pokémon the Main Pokémon belongs to, from first stage to last, shown in place of the main artwork.

**Variety**: An alternative form of the Main Pokémon (for example a Mega form), shown in place of the main artwork from the Variates button. The default form is not listed as a Variety.
